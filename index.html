About SecureSeas

SecureSeas was born from a simple idea: the maritime world deserves better technology.

Founded by a graduate student in cybersecurity and business, SecureSeas sits at the intersection of sailing, technology, and security. We're building the tools that sailors actually need — from intelligent voyage planning to modern yacht management — while laying the groundwork for a safer digital maritime future.

Whether you're a weekend sailor, a yacht owner, or a shipping company navigating an increasingly connected world, SecureSeas is here to help you stay on course.
